# 200 Download Launch Strategy

## Positioning

Primary positioning:

`AI voice estimate for suspicious voice notes`

The app should not compete with enterprise forensic deepfake tools. It should be framed as a quick consumer utility for checking suspicious voice notes, voice messages, and short recordings.

## Primary Audience

- Android users worried about AI voice cloning
- Users who receive suspicious voice notes through messengers
- Students and creators comparing real and synthetic speech
- Korean users familiar with voice phishing concerns
- English-speaking users searching for AI Voice Scam Detector tools

## Store Message

Lead with:

- Record a short sample
- Check acoustic signals
- Get an AI voice estimate
- Processed on device

Avoid leading with:

- Perfect detection
- Deepfake proof
- Legal evidence
- Security protection guarantee

## 200 Download Plan

### Phase 1: Closed Test

Target: 15-20 testers

- Friends/classmates with Android phones
- Korean student groups
- Small developer/AI KakaoTalk groups
- Ask every tester to keep opt-in for 14 days

### Phase 2: First Public Week

Target: 60-80 installs

- Post short demo videos to Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- Video idea: real voice vs AI TTS sample, then app result
- Korean title idea: `AI 목소리인지 앱으로 바로 확인해봄`
- English title idea: `Can this app catch an AI voice?`

### Phase 3: Communities

Target: 40-60 installs

- Reddit: r/AndroidApps, r/scams, r/artificial
- Korean communities: Android, AI, 보이스피싱 prevention posts where allowed
- Product Hunt or simple indie app directory post
- GitHub README with Play link

### Phase 4: Small Paid Test

Target: 50-80 installs

- Google Ads App Campaign, small budget
- Test countries: US, Korea, India, Philippines, UK
- Use keywords around AI Voice Scam Detector / deepfake voice checker
- Stop if cost per install is too high

## Review Risk Controls

- Every result screen says `Estimate only`
- Store description says results may be incorrect
- Privacy policy says no server upload for current MVP
- No call recording claim
- No police/government/official detector claim
- No medical or legal claim
